Zepto Sales Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Zepto sales data using Microsoft Power BI to understand sales performance, product demand,
revenue distribution and business trends.

The dashboard provides interactive visualizations to explore sales performance across different cities, product categories and
individual products.

2. Project Objectives

- Analyze overall revenue and total orders.
- Identify the highest revenue-generating categories.
- Analyze city-wise revenue performance.
- Identify the top 10 products based on revenue.
- Understand order distribution across categories.
- Explore the relationship between discounts and orders.
- Analyze influencer activity and its impact.

3. Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Visualization

4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Revenue: 6M
- Total Orders: 50K
- Average Discount: 4.92
- Total Products: 10

5. Dashboard Visualizations

1. Revenue by Category – Compares revenue across product categories.
2. Revenue by City – Analyzes city-wise revenue performance.
3. Top 10 Products by Revenue – Identifies the best-performing products.
4. Orders by Category – Shows order distribution across categories.
5. Discount vs Orders – Explores the relationship between discounts and orders.
6. Influencers Impact – Analyzes influencer activity and its impact.

6. Interactive Filters

- Category
- City
- Influencer Active
- Product Name

7. Key Business Insights

- Total revenue is approximately 6M.
- Total orders are approximately 50K.
- Hyderabad is the leading city by displayed revenue.
- Coca-Cola 1L is the top product by displayed revenue.
- The dashboard helps compare category-wise and city-wise performance.
